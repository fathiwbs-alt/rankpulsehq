FAQ

Quick answers.

How does the $250 every-3-days billing work?

RankPulseHQ is a recurring subscription: $250 is charged every 3 days through Whop while your plan is active. You can cancel anytime from your Whop account, and drops stop after cancellation.

What exactly is in each SEO Content Drop?

A single PDF pack containing 3 long-form researched articles (1,200–1,500 words each), a meta description for each article, and internal-link suggestions — all matched to your brand and niche.

Where are drops delivered?

Directly in your Whop chat. Every 3 days a new drop appears there — no separate dashboard, no email chains, no calls.

What do you need from me to start?

Three things: your brand name, your niche or the topics you want covered, and the tone you'd like the writing in. Send them once and drops begin.

Can I change my topics or tone later?

Yes. Message us in your Whop chat with the updated details and future drops will follow the new direction.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, from your Whop subscription settings. There's no long-term contract — you stay for as long as the drops are useful to you.
